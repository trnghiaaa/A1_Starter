Rubric
GAIT - A1 Rubric
Total Points: 20

Criteria	Ratings	Pts
This criterion is linked to a Learning Outcome Steering Behaviours
Implement realistic movement for all agents:

Arrive: Frog slows near the target and stops cleanly.

Flocking: Flies use separation, cohesion, and alignment to move as a group.

Flee: Flies escape smoothly from nearby threats.

Avoidance: Snakes steer around obstacles while chasing the frog.	6 to >4.0 pts Excellent All steering behaviours work smoothly and realistically with no jitter, overshoot, or collision issues.
4 to >2.0 pts Partially implemented Some behaviours work but lack polish or stability.
2 to >0 pts Not implemented Agents move linearly, ignore obstacles, or show no reactive motion.	6 pts
This criterion is linked to a Learning Outcome Finite State Machines (FSMs)
rogram agent states and transitions:

Flies: Switch between flocking and fleeing depending on nearby threats.

Snakes: Patrol, chase, or return home based on distance and conditions.	4 to >2.0 pts Excellent All FSM transitions function logically and produce believable behaviour.
2 to >0 pts Fair Partially implemented: Some transitions or conditions missing, causing odd state changes. Not implemented: Agents stay stuck in one state or show no reactive change.	4 pts
This criterion is linked to a Learning Outcome Advanced AI Extensions
dd creative AI enhancements beyond the basics:

Pursue / Evade: Predict movement for smarter chasing and fleeing.

Confused / Idle / Hurt states: Add extra realism with timed wandering, resting, and damage reactions.	10 to >8.0 pts Excellent Fully implemented: All extensions function as intended and enrich gameplay behaviour.
8 to >4.0 pts Good Partially implemented: Some extensions work but lack polish or stability.
4 to >0 pts Fair Not implemented: Extra behaviours not functional or missing from the game.	10 pts
